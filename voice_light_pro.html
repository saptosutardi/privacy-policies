Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the "VoiceLight Pro" application handles your data. VoiceLight Pro is designed as a privacy-first utility that provides flashlight, strobe, and ambient light functionalities controllable via voice commands. We believe that your privacy is paramount; therefore, our application is built to operate fully offline, ensuring that no personal data or voice recordings are ever collected or transmitted.

1. No Data Collection

The application does not collect, record, store, or transmit any type of personal data. We do not have servers to receive your data, and we do not use any hidden tracking mechanisms.

Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • Voice recordings or audio snippets.
  • Personal identifiers (names, emails, device IDs).
  • Location data or IP addresses.
  • Usage analytics or crash telemetry.

2. Permissions and Data Usage

To provide its core functionality, VoiceLight Pro requires access to specific device features. Both features are used strictly for real-time interaction and do not involve data storage:

  • Camera (Flashlight): Used solely to control the device's physical LED flash. We do not access the camera sensor for capturing images or videos.
  • Microphone (Voice Commands): Used to listen for commands (e.g., "On", "Off", "Strobe"). Audio processing is handled in real-time using the device's native speech recognition engine. No audio is ever recorded to disk or sent to our servers.

3. Data Security

Because VoiceLight Pro is a fully offline application, the risk of data breaches or unauthorized access via the internet is eliminated. All voice processing happens instantly on your device, and the resulting commands are executed immediately without being logged.

4. Third-Party Services

This application does not integrate with any external third-party services, including:

  • Advertising networks.
  • Cloud-based analytics (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase).
  • Third-party cloud storage or backup.

The app relies on the device's native Speech-to-Text (STT) and Text-to-Speech (TTS) engines provided by the operating system (Android or iOS).

5. Children's Privacy

Note: VoiceLight Pro is safe for all ages. It does not collect any personal information from any user, including children.

We comply with international child privacy regulations (such as COPPA and GDPR-K). Since we do not collect any data, there is no risk of children's information being misused.

6. International Compliance

VoiceLight Pro is built in accordance with global privacy standards:

  • GDPR (EU): No personal data is processed or stored.
  • CCPA (California): No data is collected, sold, or shared.
  • UU PDP 2022 (Indonesia): No personal data is processed.

7. Open-Source Notice

The transparency of this application is reflected in the availability of its source code for review. The privacy guarantees in this document apply to the official version distributed by Dr. Sapto Labs. Independent modifications or unofficial builds may have different privacy characteristics for which the original developer is not responsible.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store any data, there is no personal information to access, modify, or delete. You maintain total control over the app's permissions through your device settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect new app features or regulatory changes. Any updates will be reflected in this document and available via the app's settings or store listing.
