Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Hilal Snap" application handles your data. Our application is designed as a precision astronomical instrument that prioritizes user privacy. We are committed to protecting your information by processing data locally on your device without sending it to our external servers.

1. Data Collection and Use

This application requires access to specific device features to function as an astronomical observation tool. However, all data processing occurs locally on your device.

Specifically, we use the following data:

  • Location Data (GPS): Used ONLY to calculate the astronomical position (Azimuth, Altitude, Elongation) of the Sun and Moon based on the observer's location. Your coordinates are processed in real-time and are not stored or transmitted to our servers.
  • Camera: Used to display the Augmented Reality (AR) overlay to guide you to the celestial object's position and to capture documentation photos/videos. The camera feed is processed locally.
  • Microphone: Used only if you choose to record observation videos with audio. Audio is processed locally and stored directly into the video file on your device.
  • Device Sensors (Compass/Accelerometer/Gyroscope): Used to determine device orientation for AR accuracy and Horizon Leveling features. Sensor data is used in real-time.
  • Storage (Gallery): The application requires storage permission to save your observation photos and videos to your device's gallery.

We do NOT collect:

  • Personal information (names, email addresses, phone numbers)
  • Location history or travel data
  • Contact lists
  • User behavior analytics for advertising purposes

2. Data Security

Because this application processes data locally (offline-first) and does not transmit personal data to the cloud or external servers, the risk of data interception or privacy breach is minimal. All calculation results and media (photos/videos) remain fully under your control within your device.

3. Third-Party Services

This application does not use third-party analytics services (such as AdMob or Facebook Analytics) that track users.

  • OS System Services (Android/iOS): The application uses standard operating system services (such as Location Fusion Provider) to obtain coordinates, which are subject to the OS provider's (Google/Apple) privacy policies.

4. Permissions

For transparency, here are the permissions the application may request:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: For accurate astronomical calculations.
  • CAMERA: For AR features and image capture.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: For audio recording in videos (optional).
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES: To save photo/video results to the gallery.

5. Scientific Disclaimer

This application is an astronomical calculation tool (Hisab) and observation aid (Rukyat). Calculation results are based on the precise JPL DE440 algorithm. However, the determination of the beginning of Hijri months (such as Ramadan/Shawwal) follows the official decision of the government or local religious authorities (Isbat Session). Users are advised to use this application as a supporting reference.

6. Children's Privacy

This application is safe for use by all ages, including children under supervision for science/astronomy educational purposes. We do not collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as application features evolve. Changes will be informed through updates on this page or within the latest application release.
